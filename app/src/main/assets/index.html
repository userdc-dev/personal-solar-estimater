Location

Search your suburb or enter coordinates. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo for weather; no account is needed.

Your starting template: SAJ H2 10 kW and 30 × 440 W panels. The 13% loss assumption includes inverter, temperature, wiring and dirt losses; it is not applied again elsewhere. Adjust using several weeks of readings.

Panel groups

Direction is clockwise from north: N 0°, E 90°, S 180°, W 270°. Tilt: 0° flat, 90° vertical.

How this works

Tilted solar radiation is requested separately for every panel group. Panel capacity, your combined loss allowance and group shading are applied, then total AC output is capped at the inverter rating.

Bifacial rear-side gain is not separately added. Shading is a fixed percentage, not a tree/building simulation. A DC-coupled battery may capture energy beyond this simplified AC estimate. Weather refreshes while the app is open; cached forecasts stay available offline and show their age.
